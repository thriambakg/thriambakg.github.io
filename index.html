02

In progress · rdistaging.com

Founder · Mar 2026 – Present

RDI

Remote Drone Interfaces: low-latency piloting from a browser to a relay in the field.

  • Operators use a React console. Commands, telemetry, and video move on a WebRTC connection between the browser and a Raspberry Pi CM4 relay. Amazon Kinesis Video Streams is the signaling channel only. After the session connects, the payload does not hairpin through Kinesis.
  • Field relays are backhauled with Starlink. That path was chosen over AWS Wavelength so a session does not depend on a carrier edge zone staying available through local weather or a disrupted link.
  • The control plane is API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, Cognito, and CloudFront, assembled from Terraform modules and CI/CD I already operated. Reuse took months off the build. On the vehicle side, Rust and Python agents speak MAVLink to PX4 and ArduPilot-class autopilots, including a radio hop past the relay when the aircraft is beyond the local link.
